# Utsav House — Luxury Event Planner Website

Official website for **Utsav House**, premier event planners across Jaipur and Rajasthan, crafting stylish celebrations from royal weddings and themed birthdays to high-impact corporate events and catering.

## Project Structure
- **`Utsav_House_Event_Planner_Website/`**: Main responsive multi-page website
  - `index.html` — Home page
  - `weddings.html` — Wedding planning services & real photo showcase
  - `birthday-celebrations.html` — Themed birthday parties & milestone gallery
  - `wedding-moments.html` — Wedding celebration moments collection
  - `wedding-reception.html` — Royal receptions & night lighting
  - `services.html` — Full service overview
  - `gallery.html` — Visual gallery collections
  - `stage-production.html` — Stage, lighting & production
  - `venue-styling.html` — Decor & tabletop styling
  - `live-event-experience.html` — Live entertainment & guest energy
  - `corporate-events.html` & `corporate-gathering.html` — Business events
  - `catering.html` & `curated-dining.html` — Culinary & dining experiences
  - `blog.html` — Event trends & guides
  - `contact.html` — Event booking & inquiry form
  - `assets/` — Stylesheets, scripts, royal logo, and high-resolution event media
